# Sales Performance Dashboard 📊

## Project Overview
An interactive sales analytics dashboard built in Power BI, analysing 51,000+ orders across multiple regions, product categories, and time periods. The goal was to transform raw transactional sales data into a clear, decision-ready report that allows business stakeholders to monitor performance and identify opportunities.

## Tools Used
- **Power BI** — dashboard development, DAX, interactive filters
- **Microsoft Excel** — data cleaning and preparation
- **Dataset** — Superstore Orders Dataset (Kaggle)

## Business Questions This Project Answers
- Which regions are generating the most revenue?
- How has sales performance trended over time?
- Which product categories are most and least profitable?
- What is the overall profit margin across the business?

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards** — Total Profit (R1.47M), Total Sales (R7.84M), Number of Orders (51.29K), Profit Margin (19%)
- **Sales by Region** — horizontal bar chart ranking all regions by total sales
- **Sales Over Time** — line chart showing sales trend across the full date range
- **Sales by Category** — breakdown across Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture
- **Dynamic Filters** — interactive slicers for region, category, and order date

## Key Findings
- 📌 Central region generates the highest sales across all regions
- 📌 Sales show a gradual decline over time indicating a trend worth investigating
- 📌 Office Supplies leads all categories in total revenue
- 📌 Overall profit margin sits at 19% across 51,000+ orders
